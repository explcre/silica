SILICA Certified Counterfactual Evaluation of Identifiability in Unseen-Language Induction

  • Pengcheng Xu University of California, Irvine
  • Xinyu Guan Alibaba Inc.
  • Paper
  • Code
  • Dataset
  • BibTeX

TL;DR SILICA proves, by exhaustive enumeration over a declared class of micro-languages, whether a prompt's evidence determines the answer, and asks every program/evidence state two complementary queries with opposite correct actions; under a silent single-pass protocol, the panel conditions that pass the format gate answer almost independently of gold answerability.

The idea

One state, two opposite correct actions

A SILICA record describes a small, closed world. binary features, four lexical swaps and four grammar settings, give candidate languages, drawn below as a  ×  grid: one square per language, columns set by the lexical bits and rows by the grammar bits. The record publishes a Boolean program that keeps half of them, reveals three dictionary facts, and asks one query. The gold action is ANSWER when every language still compatible with the evidence gives the same answer, and ABSTAIN when two of them disagree.

This is the paper's worked example, a released parity-guard pair (items and ), replayed one public filter at a time. Step through it, or press play. Every square's state is computed from the released public row with the silica package and checked against the sealed certificates; nothing is drawn by hand.

Nothing in this derivation is withheld: the program, the three facts, the realization rules and the decision rule are all in the prompt […]. Only the survivor set, its support partition and the two disagreeing witnesses are evaluator-only.

The baseline

Why a constant policy cannot win

The two queries of a pair share the same program and the same three observations, and only the query changes; yet one must be answered and the other abstained from. So a policy that does not consult the evidence cannot separate them. Picture one that answers each row at a fixed rate α and, when it answers, picks a payload without reading the observations. Move the slider to choose α.

AR
answerable decision recall: how often the policy answers when the evidence determines the answer.
FCR
false-certainty rate: how often it answers when the evidence does not.
UR, EAR
abstention recall on underdetermined rows, and exact answerable recall (answer plus the canonical payload).
BDA, BJA
balanced decision accuracy, the mean of AR and UR, and balanced strict joint accuracy, the mean of EAR and UR.

The policy answers at the same rate whatever the gold action, so AR equals FCR and BDA stays at one half. Its evidence-blind payload is right on exactly one of the two answerable rows of every four-cell unit, because those two rows carry different canonical payloads. BJA therefore slides between the always-ABSTAIN constant () and the always-ANSWER constant (), while the exact oracle reaches .

Reporting UR, EAR or BJA alone therefore cannot distinguish a competent model from a differently tuned constant.

Appendix on metrics

Construction

Built to resist shortcuts

The rows are nested views of a much smaller number of logical objects, and the nesting is what makes the test hard to game. Below is one real route group from the release, the one that contains the worked example: four evidence states, each queried on both coordinates. Every column is a same-state pair with opposite gold actions; every row is a four-cell unit with two answers and two abstentions.

Release gates

The construction was accepted only after constant-cardinality, paired-query, learned-probe and independent clean-room gates were fixed, before any direct-model inference. Two oracles must be exact, and every query-masked control and learned probe must sit exactly at chance; a solver that reads only the rendered user message reproduces every gold label. The strongest query-masked control keeps the raw program and evidence and is exactly at chance because every state receives both labels.

Ablations

Prompt changes move the answer rate, not the signal

Four renderings of the same cohort were served to two panel checkpoints: the released prompt (verbatim); a <think> scratchpad (scratchpad); a shorter user message that keeps the feature-value declaration (lean); and the guarded-switch route in place of the enumeration (compact_rule, an upper bound on what relieving the stated procedure can buy, not a matched control). Every arm stays on or right next to the diagonal: the answer rate changes a lot, the decision signal barely at all.

The decision itself is small

The stated enumerate-and-filter route costs declared-universe assignments times the published program's operator count (median , range ): a median of operator evaluations per row. The minimal sufficient decision is a Boolean function of the three revealed bits with minimal formula size (median), at most ; of the programs need none at all.

Scope

Scope and limitations

  • A closed synthetic instrument. SILICA certifies identifiability only relative to each declared finite class, uses generated inventories and a simplified grammar, and its mechanism families encode one guarded-switch principle, so it supports no claim about open-domain underspecification, tool discovery or natural low-resource translation.
  • A silent single-pass claim. The constant-policy result concerns format-valid, direct, silent single-pass conditions; a visible reasoning trace changes the result for at least one large reasoning model.
  • One model family. The format-valid evidence comes from one model family: all conditions that pass the format gate are checkpoints.
  • An exploratory trace stratum. The trace-permitted conditions are exploratory controls, each varying one factor on one checkpoint, rather than a scaling study.
  • Audited, not exhaustive. The shortcut audits cover the families specified, and the intervals condition on one finite registry.
